## Short description

Manage gym members, memberships, fees and outstanding dues.

## Full description

MuscleDesk helps gym owners and administrators keep member and fee records organized in one workspace.

MEMBER RECORDS
Create and update member profiles, find members by code, name or phone, and keep membership details easy to review. Add an optional profile photo or use the initials fallback.

MEMBERSHIPS AND PLANS
Manage plan prices and durations. Review membership dates, current status and expiry, and create, renew or cancel memberships through the supported workflows.

FEES AND OUTSTANDING DUES
Record full or partial fee payments, review payment history and transaction references, and see remaining balances. Outstanding-dues views help you identify records that need attention. Amounts are presented in Indian rupees.

GYM OVERVIEW
Use the Dashboard to review recorded revenue, member statistics and outstanding dues. Open the detailed revenue and member records when you need more information.

TRAINERS AND GYM PROFILE
Maintain trainer profiles and your owner/gym contact information. Use the app's light and dark themes while reviewing daily operations.

MuscleDesk is an owner/admin gym-management tool. It records payments collected by your gym; it does not process card, bank or UPI transfers through an in-app payment gateway. An internet connection is needed for server-backed operations. Supported recovery flows help review interrupted operations, rather than promising general offline synchronization.
